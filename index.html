Creating Awareness. Building Resilience. Supporting Mental Well-being.

ManoUday is rooted in the belief that awareness is the first step towards change.

ManoUday works to make mental health conversations empathetic, accessible, and stigma-free across rural, semi-urban, and urban communities in India.

Mental Health in India – Snapshot

  • 1 in 7 people in India experience mental health concerns.
  • 80–85% of individuals may not receive adequate treatment.
  • <1 professional per 100,000 people in many regions.

Source: National Mental Health Survey (NIMHANS), WHO.

Understanding the Need

Across communities, many individuals continue to struggle silently with mental health concerns due to stigma, low awareness, and limited access to support. Children face academic pressure, adolescents grapple with identity and social stressors, and adults navigate occupational and personal burdens often without safe emotional spaces.

  • Persistent stigma and misconceptions around mental health.
  • Limited access to affordable and trained professionals.
  • Lack of early identification in schools and communities.
  • Increasing stress, anxiety, and substance use concerns.

About ManoUday

ManoUday is a people-centered, socially driven mental health awareness and outreach initiative founded by Ms. Prateeksha Runwal. It is committed to bridging the gap in understanding, acceptance, and access to mental health support through community engagement, education, and evidence-informed practices.

Vision

To foster a society where mental health is understood with empathy, addressed without stigma, and supported with care.

Mission

To create awareness, enable early identification, and provide accessible psychological support through outreach, counselling, and psychoeducation.

Approach

Cultural sensitivity, ethical practice, practical interventions, and collaborative action with schools, institutions, and communities.

Our Objectives

Promoting Awareness

Open conversations through workshops and outreach.

Early Identification

Timely support for emotional and behavioural concerns.

Supportive Communities

Empathy-led responses from educators, parents, and peers.

Resilience Building

Coping skills and emotional well-being practices.

Approachable counselling, guidance, and psychoeducation.

School & College Programs

Helplines

ManoUday is not a crisis-based mental health service, and counselling support may not be available for urgent situations outside of scheduled sessions. If you or someone you know is experiencing a mental health emergency or requires immediate assistance, please contact emergency services or dedicated helplines.

Legal & Ethical Notice

ManoUday is a mental health awareness and outreach initiative. Services and content are educational, supportive, and preventive in nature, and are not a substitute for medical diagnosis or psychiatric treatment.

Personal information is handled with confidentiality and ethical care, with limitations in situations of risk of harm to self/others as applicable under law and professional guidelines.
